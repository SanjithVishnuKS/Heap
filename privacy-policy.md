# Heap Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 22, 2026_

This starter policy must be reviewed by a qualified professional before public release.

## Prototype

The current Heap prototype stores thoughts, counters, and beta events in the browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a Heap server by this prototype. Browser speech recognition may send audio or transcripts to the browser vendor's recognition service according to that vendor's policy.

## Production plan

Before production, Heap must clearly disclose what data is stored, which AI provider processes it, retention periods, subprocessors, encryption, account deletion, data export, and contact details. AI provider keys must remain server-side. Users must consent before voice or third-party AI processing.

## User rights

The production service should provide access, correction, export, and deletion controls. Do not invite external beta users until hosting, contact details, terms, and deletion procedures are in place.
